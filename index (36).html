What we do

Everything a facilitator needs, in one app

ClassTrack replaces the stack of paper, photos and group chats. Facilitators teach, mark and keep records. Learners see their work and results.

Worksheets and books

Publish multi-page worksheets and workbooks. Every learner in the class receives them instantly.

Red-pen marking

Mark on the learner's own page with ticks, crosses, notes and your saved signature, by finger, mouse or stylus.

Learner portfolios

Handed-in and marked work is kept in each learner's portfolio, ready to download as a PDF.

Attendance register

Learners check in, and you keep a register you can download as a PDF.

Made for learners' phones

Learners sign in with their own email, turn pages like a book, and write on the answer lines.

Live online classes

Teach live with video and voice. Small groups are included, and LiveKit online classes handle a full class.

How it works

From worksheet to marked portfolio

1

Publish

The facilitator publishes a worksheet to the class.

2

Write

Learners receive it at once and write their answers on their phones.

3

Mark

The facilitator marks with the red pen, ticks, notes and a signature.

4

Keep

Marked work lands in the learner's portfolio, ready as a PDF.
